DayAtlas Help

SUMMARY
• Location is logged in the background; data stays on the phone (optional Drive backup).
• With Daily mode on you do not tap Start — recording continues automatically.
• Map: bottom of the Daily tab; most of the screen on full day detail.
• The Jumps button is always under the map (“No jumps” when the day is clean).
• Tap a Statistics bar or a Routes row to open day detail.
• Help: More → Help. Language and sample interval are dropdowns in Settings.

QUICK START

1. Grant location “Allow all the time” (needed with the screen off).
2. Turn on Daily mode in Settings — recording starts on its own.
3. Allow notifications and, if offered, battery exemption; on OEM phones also enable autostart.

1) RECORDING MODES

Daily mode (recommended): recording resumes whenever the app or phone starts; Start/Stop is hidden. Turn Daily mode off to stop.

Manual mode: with Daily mode off, use Start/Stop yourself (handy for a single trip).

2) SAMPLE INTERVAL

Choose 30 s / 1 / 3 / 5 min from the Settings dropdown; 1 min suits most use. Three samples in the same place (~80 m) coarsen the interval one step; real outdoor motion (two consecutive moves) restores your choice. Indoor GPS jitter does not tighten the interval again — that protects overnight battery.

3) PERMISSIONS AND BATTERY

You need: location “all the time”, notifications, and preferably battery exemption. Settings buttons open the matching system screens. Xiaomi / Huawei / Oppo / Samsung may also need autostart; a dedicated button appears when known. Without these, recording can stop quietly after reboot or long idle.

4) MAP AND DAY STATS

Daily tab: selected day’s route at the bottom; arrows change day, “today” jumps back. Past days show distance / last point / count over the map.

Today’s tiles: distance, last point, GPS check count, interval, speeds, active time. Long-press to reorder; Settings → “Map day stats” chooses which tiles show.

Day detail (full screen): opened from Statistics or Routes. The map uses most of the screen (even wider on the left in landscape); under it sit Jumps, the note icon, photos, and stats.

5) GPS JUMPS — WHERE IS THE BUTTON?

Where: directly under the map on the Daily tab and on day detail. Always visible.
• If jumps exist: “Jumps (N) — tap to delete”
• If none: “No jumps” (tap shows a short explanation)

What it does: weak GPS can write an implausible point. Two layers:
1) Hard teleports (~30 km / extreme speed) are never written.
2) Mid-range suspects are held one sample; if the next fix returns home, the soft spike is dropped.
Remaining jumps are orange on the map and deletable from the button; distance recalculates.

6) STATS AND ROUTES

Statistics: tap a daily distance bar → day detail; Back → Statistics.
Routes: tap a day → same detail; Back → Routes. Row export arrow = one-day GPX; “Range GPX” for a date span.

7) GPX EXPORT

Standard route file — Google Earth, Strava, Komoot, etc. Share one day or a range from Routes.

8) GOOGLE DRIVE BACKUP

Pick a folder in Settings (empty Drive folder ideal), enable daily backup. Each day uploads once as GPX. On a new phone: same folder + “Restore from backup”.

9) UPDATES

Daily background check; downloads and notifies to install. Settings → “Check for updates”. Sideload installs may need “unknown sources”.

10) LANGUAGE

Settings → Language dropdown (TR/EN/DE/ZH/HI/ES/FR/AR or system). Chips on this Help page only change this screen.

11) DATA AND PRIVACY

Data lives under files/days/ on the phone only. No ads or analytics. Network use is OpenStreetMap tiles for the visible area. With Drive backup off, nothing leaves the device.

12) KNOWN LIMITATIONS

Some OEMs (Samsung One UI) may show a recents card while backgrounded — recording is unaffected. On aggressive battery devices, open the app once a day to keep logging reliable.

FAQ

Does closing the app stop recording?
No — with Daily mode and correct permissions it continues in the background.

Why did I not see Jumps / why does it say “No jumps”?
The button is under the map. If there is no hard jump left to delete it shows “No jumps”; soft spikes may already have been dropped automatically.

Do my data go to the internet?
Not unless you turn on Drive backup.

How do I move history to a new phone?
Backup on the old phone → same folder on the new one → restore.

A bad GPS point ruined distance?
Delete it from Jumps under the map; distance recalculates.
